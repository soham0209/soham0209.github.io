abstract: 'Denoising noisy datasets is a crucial task in this data-driven world. In this paper, we develop a persistence-guided discrete Morse theoretic denoising framework. We use our method to denoise point-clouds and to extract surfaces from noisy volumes. In addition, we show that our method generally outperforms standard methods. Our paper is a synergy of classical noise removal techniques and topological data analysis.'
